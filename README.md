### Bayesian Machine Learning Project: Bayesian and Frequentist Models for Rental Price Prediction and Market Segmentation

This project investigated rental price determinants and market heterogeneity through the Apart-
ments for Rent Classified dataset from the UCI Machine Learning Repository. Linear, Ridge,
Lasso, and Bayesian linear regression models were used to predict rental prices relative to city-
level median rents. In addition, Gaussian Mixture Models and Bayesian Gaussian Mixture Models
were applied for cluster analysis, to identify latent rental submarkets based on structural property
characteristics. The results showed that Bayesian and regularised models achieved similar predic-
tive performance to ordinary least squares due to the large sample size. The clustering analysis
revealed substantial heterogeneity across rental market segments, and cluster-specific regressions
showed that the effects of amenities on rental prices vary across submarkets. In several clusters,
cluster-specific Bayesian regressions also achieved lower prediction errors than the pooled regression
model, suggesting that modelling relatively homogeneous submarkets can improve local predictive
performance. Overall, the findings suggest that combining Bayesian regression with probabilistic
clustering provides a useful framework for analysing heterogeneous rental housing markets.
